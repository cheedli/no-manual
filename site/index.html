Questions about No Manual.

Do I need anything besides photos?

A tape measure helps the solver. Put an A4 sheet or a known object in the panel photos and it can read sizes from the pictures. For the manual finder, a photo of any label or stamped number is the fastest route.

What furniture does it work on?

The manual finder and recorder work on almost anything. The solver handles carcass furniture: closets, wardrobes, bookcases, cabinets and nightstands. Drawers with runners are on the roadmap.

Why won't it help with my crib or bunk bed?

A wrong guess there can hurt someone. For children's furniture and anything people sit, sleep, stand or climb on, it only finds the official manual. That rule lives in the tool code and a hook, so no prompt can talk it out of it.

What happens when it isn't sure?

Every step has a confidence. Below 0.6 it asks a targeted question instead of answering. Between 0.6 and 0.75 the step gets an amber "Check this step" badge and Hermes asks for a photo.

Where do my photos go?

To your own model provider through Hermes, to read panels and labels. They're stored in your Hermes data folder and removed with /nomanual delete. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

Which model does it need?

Any vision-capable model you've configured in Hermes. All model calls go through Hermes, so the plugin has no keys or accounts of its own.

How are the step pictures made?

Drawn from the geometry of your actual parts with plain code, never by an image model. That's why the drawing always matches the panel in your hands.

Does it work in Telegram?

Yes. Steps arrive as images in Telegram, WhatsApp and the other chat apps Hermes connects to. Hermes Desktop adds the interactive 3D viewer.

What does it cost?

Nothing. It's MIT-licensed. You only pay your usual model usage through Hermes.
